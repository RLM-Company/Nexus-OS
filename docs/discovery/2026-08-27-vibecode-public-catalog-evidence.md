## Evidence limits

The screenshots do not establish exact context/output limits except the non-exact `>272K` note for
GPT 5.6 Sol. They also do not establish protocol, structured output, tool calling, upstream training
use, region, contractual retention, or lifecycle guarantees. `Online` is a point-in-time catalog
status, not a production availability SLA.

The public homepage says request contents and chat history are not stored, while the public privacy
policy says interaction history may be collected and used for service analysis and improvement.
Until VibeCode resolves that conflict contractually, retention and training usage remain `unknown`,
and no route is confidential-compatible.
